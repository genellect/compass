# Habitat supporting assets

Revision 3 uses original COMPASS architecture, furniture, camera paths and assembly
scripts together with the following supporting assets. These assets are not
represented as original COMPASS photography or modelling.

| Asset | Creator | Source | License | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marble 01 | Rob Tuytel | https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01 | CC0 | Floor colour, roughness and normal maps |
| Rough Linen | colormass (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen | CC0 | Upholstery colour, roughness and normal maps |
| Potted Plant 01 | Rico Cilliers | https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_01 | CC0 | Decimated foliage and stem; original pot and pebbles excluded |
| Modern Arm Chair 01 | Vibrant Nordic | https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01 | CC0 | Upholstered lounge seating; frame assigned a satin titanium material |

License verified 2026-09-09: https://polyhaven.com/license . CC0 permits commercial
use, modification and redistribution. Only downloadable assets are included;
the website's preview renders and branding are excluded.

Downloads are build-time authoring inputs, kept outside `public/`. The website
does not call Poly Haven's API. The master `.blend` packs the inputs required
for editing; optimised GLBs embed only textures actually used by their materials.

The Earth surface map is **NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation**,
July 2004, by Reto Stöckli. Source and republishing credit guidance:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/ .
The distribution map is resized from the 5400 × 2700 global JPEG:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/july/world.topo.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg .
It represents the real Earth surface within an invented COMPASS architectural scene;
NASA does not endorse COMPASS or this scene.
