# Molecular artwork data

`1BNA.pdb` is an unmodified download from
https://files.rcsb.org/download/1BNA.pdb (retrieved 2026-10-10).

Entry: https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1BNA
PDB archive license: CC0 1.0, https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
SHA-256: `df42f1506792f191b957227b061360652adcf6f813eb69d9ec553067ea584670`

Drew et al. (1981), *Structure of a B-DNA dodecamer: conformation and dynamics*,
PNAS 78:2179–2183. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179

The offline Blender renderer uses the 486 non-water ATOM coordinates. It does
not distort the molecule. Bond strokes are inferred from within-chain distances
under 1.9 angstroms, not a formal chemical bond-order annotation. Independent
materials, placement of multiple coordinate instances, light and camera motion
are artistic, not measured specimen relationships or biological simulation.
This is science-inspired artwork, not a Cytellect result or performance claim.
No Lila.ai assets are included.
